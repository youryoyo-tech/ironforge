About the club

More than a gym.
It's a movement.

Ironforge started in 2015 as a single squat rack in a Harrison Street warehouse. Ten years later we're an 18,000 sq ft coach-led club — but the rule hasn't changed: every member gets a plan, a coach who knows their name, and a room that expects them back tomorrow.

We cap our class sizes, keep the floor spotless, and program in twelve-week blocks so progress is measurable rather than accidental.

Why Ironforge

Built for people who want results

Six reasons members stay with us an average of 3.4 years — well over double the industry norm.

  • Expert Trainers

    Certified coaches with an average of 11 years on the floor. Every member gets a movement screen before their first session.

  • Premium Equipment

    Eleven power racks, calibrated plates, competition benches, sleds and a full turf lane — replaced on a five-year cycle.

  • Flexible Memberships

    Month-to-month plans from $29 with no lock-in contract. Pause or switch tiers whenever your training changes.

  • Personal Training

    One-to-one coaching with written programming, video form reviews and check-ins between your sessions.

  • Group Classes

    Over 50 classes a week, capped at 16–24 people so a coach can actually see and correct every rep.

  • Supportive Community

    Member socials, in-house lifting meets and a floor culture where the strongest lifter re-racks their own plates.

Programs

Classes for every kind of strong

Eight programs run every week, each capped so your coach can see every rep. Scaled options are posted before the clock starts.

  • All Levels

    Strength Training

    60 min Alex Johnson

    Barbell-based sessions built around squat, hinge, press and pull. Every block is programmed in waves so you can see your numbers climb week after week.

  • Intermediate

    HIIT

    45 min Maya Torres

    Short, sharp intervals on rowers, bikes and the turf. Big output, structured rest, and a coach counting every round so you never have to guess your pace.

  • Intermediate

    Cross Training

    60 min Maya Torres

    Mixed-modal workouts that stitch together barbells, gymnastics and machines. Scaled options are posted for every movement before the clock starts.

  • Beginner

    Yoga & Mobility

    50 min Hana Lee

    A slow, deliberate flow focused on hips, shoulders and thoracic spine — the three areas that limit most lifters. Ideal on rest days or after heavy sessions.

  • All Levels

    Boxing

    50 min Darius Cole

    Wraps on, rounds of technical bag work, footwork ladders and defence drills. Real boxing fundamentals with conditioning built into the rounds.

  • All Levels

    Functional Fitness

    45 min Alex Johnson

    Sled pushes, carries, kettlebell work and crawling patterns on the turf lane. Training that transfers directly to how you move outside the gym.
